POSIX only, and Windows returns `Err`): `std.process.Command.new(program: &str) -> Command` is a container for settings, and `program` is searched from `PATH`. `Command` is a linear type (consumed by one of `spawn` / `output` / `cancel`. For the child's standard output in `ksn run`, the parent's output is flushed before starting and then inherited.

Types containing `Vec` / `String` / `Option` / enum payloads are outside KIR, and fall back to the bundle with a displayed reason.

## 9. Decision log (conclusions of the open items of v0.4)

1. Recursion through indirect calls: the stack upper bound is statically computed on a call graph that includes all candidate functions (5.3).
2. Operator precedence: settled by the precedence of 4.6. Inner blocks are `{ }`.
3. Associated types: not provided. Expressed by the trait's type parameters (3.2).
4. Threads and Send/Sync: settled in 5.5. Channels etc. are `std.sync` of 8.2.
5. `kasane.toml` / `kasane.lock`: settled in 8.1.
6. Language name, extension, and command name: a collision survey was done, and the current names are settled (chapter 8). The formal trademark survey is before publication.
7. The API signatures of the standard library are normatively `spec/stdlib/` (`.ksnl` / `.sig`) and are mechanically verified with `spec/tools/check_all.py` (8.3, 8.4).
8. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-1 Asynchrony: suspension is introduced as the effect `uses [suspend]`. No coloring of functions (`async` / `await`) (5.6. Implemented from 0.10.15. Chapter 10).
9. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-2 Width of `usize` / `isize`: target-dependent (x86_64 and aarch64 are 64-bit, wasm32 is 32-bit). A `usize` constant that does not fit the target's width is an out-of-range literal error (4.5. No warning is created. Implemented in 0.10.26: range checks of `const` / `static` / array lengths. In 0.10.30, associated constants such as `core.num.usize.MAX` / `BITS` became the target's values, and conversions that can lose values on 32-bit became `try_from_*`).
10. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-3 Orphan rule: relaxed so that an `impl` can also be placed in the module that defines the trait (another assignee writes the specification and implementation).
11. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-4 Formatting API: a macro-less builder form is added to `core.fmt`. The treatment of comparison and display of `String` is also reviewed together (another assignee).
12. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-5 Serialization: JSON input and output is added to `derive`. Presupposes 4-3 (implemented: `derive [ToJson, FromJson]` and `std.json`. 4.3 / 8.3. Because trait methods require `self` (3.2), `FromJson` is a marker trait + the associated function `T.from_json`).
13. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-6 Omitting effect variables: the effect arguments of `Fn*` bounds can be omitted (another assignee).
14. Decision (2026-11-14) 4-7 `TryClone`: made a trait, and `derive [TryClone]` is added (another assignee).
15. Decision (2026-11-14) 3-3 Foundation of `Vec` / `String`: no unsafe primitive operations (`from_raw_parts`, etc.) are added, and the implementation holds, as a built-in, exactly one safe box `alloc.raw.Buf<T, A>` (8.3). `Vec` / `String` are written on top of it in Kasane without unsafe.
16. Decision (2026-11-14) `_` in `match` on an `enum` stays forbidden (enforcing full enumeration, preserving the property that adding a variant stops every `match` in checking). `if let`, which looks at only one shape, and `expr is pattern` are added (4.6. Item 6 of chapter 6 of the self-hosting prototype `selfhost/README.md`).
17. Decision (2026-11-14) Destruction of recursive types: `derive [Drop]` is added, and the implementation generates destruction that does not depend on depth (makes no recursive calls). The guarantee that the stack does not overflow is kept (5.3, E4013. Item 7 of chapter 6 of `selfhost/README.md`).
18. Decision (2026-11-15) 4-1 Stage 1 of asynchrony (the "Decision (stage 1)" of 5.6): the cooperative executor `std.task` (implemented in the tree walker. One OS thread per task, with a signal that is one at a time), borrows across suspension are E3050 (conservative), no cancellation, the caller of `block_on` also declares `suspend`, and native is E9040 with no fallback (later lowered natively in stage 2 (0.10.18), and cancellation added in stage 4 (0.10.28). `suspend` is required only when the closure passed suspends. See the decisions of stage 2 and stage 4 in 5.6). The wait for a task is `join_task` (distinguished by name from `JoinHandle.join`).
19. Decision (2026-11-15) Making `extern` and `core.ptr` functions into values: they are not treated as values (3.8). They cannot be put into variables, fields, closures, return values, or `if let` bindings even inside `unsafe`, closing the hole through which they could be called from outside as ordinary functions. The 2026-11-15 "references as values inside `unsafe` are accepted" is withdrawn and rejected with E5003.
20. Decision (2026-11-15) Documentation: the key that points to a definition is the FQN. Line numbers are written neither in sources nor in documents, and `ksn` computes them at each output (so they do not shift with edits or the reordering of `ksn fmt`). Links inside `///` are FQNs (3.2). The index for LLMs is `ksn index` (7). Code examples (`ksn` fences) are run by `ksn test` (3.2). A module description is written with a leading `///` and one blank line (an exception of the grammar's lexical layer. `spec/grammar.ebnf`).

- 0.10.33: replaced the 41 provisional diagnostic numbers with the codes the implementation emits (the mapping of diagnostics is authoritatively `ksn explain`). Wrote the static promotion of literals in 4.5.
- 0.10.32: all value-discarding expression statements are now E2042 (4.6). A call of a function or method (including `Result`), an `if` with `else`, a `match` statement, or a value at the tail of a loop body is E2042 if other than `()` / `!`. A `Result` is propagated with `?`, and to discard it use `core.mem.drop(..)`.
- 0.10.31: made the implicit conversions of 4.2 strict, as in the table (review round 4). `&str` cannot be passed in the position of `&String` (E2020 / E2023). A closure with captures cannot be passed to a `fn(..)` type (E2023). `Option<&[T; N]>` cannot be passed to an argument of `Option<&[T]>` (E2023). Stated "`&mut [T; N]`→`&mut [T]`" and "`&mut T`→`&dyn Tr` (composition with weakening)" explicitly in the table. When passing a named function to an `Fn` bound, the declared effects are also compared with the bound (E4001 / E4002), and the effects of a value passed to an inferred generic function with an omitted bound are borne by the caller (4.3).
- 0.10.29: stage 5 of suspension `suspend` (stage 4 of 修正プラン 4-1, the "Decision (stage 5, await_js)" of 5.6, chapter 26 of 設計_KIR.md). Added `std.task.await_js(handle: u32) -> i32 uses [suspend] panics never` (wasm32 only). Implemented the ABI in which `ksn_main` returns -1 when waiting for JS completion and JS resumes with `ksn_resume(handle, value) -> i32` ("suspension waiting on a JS Promise (await_js)" of 7.2. The storage is `ksn.js.tab`, with `ksn.js.put` / `ksn.js.take`). In the tree walker it is a panic (no JS counterpart), and in native `--release` it is E9040. The example `tools/wasm/await` runs on node.
